# Product-driven scaffold discovery

Desired open-side dimensions must be expressible without requiring callers to
translate them into an adapter annealing minimum. An outer strict request owns
that translation and explicit method permissions. It delegates every branch to
the existing [complete-options evaluator](0042-complete-scaffold-options.md);
it does not add another chemistry engine or weaken a single-enzyme release contract.

`models.construction.discovery` owns `hop.scaffold-discovery/v1`.
`design.construction.discovery` owns preview, finite branch expansion, orchestration,
physical grouping and saved-packet admission. The export layer consumes verified
facts and existing pairing/stage drawings. It does not infer reaction outcomes.

Scaffold ranges intersect inherited hard requirements. Adapter redesign requires
explicit permission and an unfixed adapter policy. Candidate minima account for
allowed nick-to-stem offsets, but are not asserted to equal achieved pairing.
Each branch must still pass whole-route requirements. Explicit release alternatives
replace release and corresponding basal end-generation permissions; forbidden,
reserved and unknown identities produce conflicts. Other roles and material
constraints remain active. No outer method declaration preserves inner permissions.

Expansion has a 256-branch ceiling before enumeration; execution evaluates an
ordered prefix of at most 64 branches and 64 local combinations per branch.
Local search completion, recorded pair coverage, rejected continuations and
unexamined branches remain distinct. Branch identity hashes its full strict
inner request; changing only the outer budget does not rename existing branches.
Catalog identity remains distinct from physical product identity and stock.

Physical grouping hashes oriented final strands and chemistry, indexed pairing
contacts, product topology and oriented interfaces. Manufacturing lineage and
precursor cut coordinates are excluded. Continuous strand records encode the
result of ligation; historical join origin does not create a different physical
chain. No rotation or strand-permutation equivalence is inferred. Direct hairpins
use their verified hairpin contacts at both stages. Product-stage hashes are
comparison keys, never replacements for verified construction identities.

The `hop.molecular-signature/v2` convention treats each pairing contact as an
undirected edge between oriented strand/base addresses. Reversing the recorded
endpoints does not create another substrate. Strand order and direction, base
letters, contact class, chemistry and cohesive ends remain identity-bearing.
Directional covalent bonds and manufacturing ancestry are not reordered.

Earlier unversioned fingerprints retained contact endpoint notation and could
split one hairpin into several product groups. The version tag enters the hash,
so v2 fingerprints must not be compared with legacy fingerprints. Existing
producer-bound checkpoints fail closed after a producer change; frozen packets
remain reproducible with their pinned producer. Reassessment under v2 creates a
new derived result from replayed authorities, retaining every preparation and
the original packet. Do not rewrite saved selections or historical counts in
place.

Saved packets use canonical manifests, existing bounded capture and create-only
publication. Admission recompiles the authored preview, requires every recorded
branch and budget to agree, replays inner local/complete authorities and regenerates
the summary. Resealing a changed summary cannot confer authority. Named selection
regenerates molecular drawings from admitted records; untrusted SVG never feeds
rendering. Ordinary exact `set.json` remains the portable authority. Stage grouping
is an additional projection, and shared-property set controls retain their existing
hairpin semantics.

Opening reports disclose specific recorded failures and missing coverage without
inventing site coordinates or unique repairs. Successful branches can be shown as
explicit alternatives. Whole-molecule comparison, changed open-side positions,
shared released product and material changes share one report vocabulary.

The [user workflow](../../guides/discover-product-scaffolds.md) and
[delivery plan](../../dev/plans/product-first-discovery.md) specify acceptance.
Joint search, arbitrary new molecular endpoints, experimental performance and
unfamiliar-user comprehension remain separate evidence questions.
