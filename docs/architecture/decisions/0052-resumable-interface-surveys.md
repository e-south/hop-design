# Resumable complete-interface surveys

The small-roster discovery packet is useful for inspecting a few requests. Its
entry limits, prefix continuation and copied drawings cannot define the scope of
an interface-coverage study. A four-base interface domain contains 256 words per
side and 65,536 ordered pairs. All targets must remain accounted for even when a
single invocation evaluates only part of that domain.

The survey must account for end-pair targets, local sequence choices and complete
preparations separately. The sections below explain how work is scheduled, how
conditional auxiliary sequences are completed, and how saved results establish
coverage.

## Decision

Add a resumable survey over the existing molecular authorities.
`models/construction/surveys` owns the strict request and target addressing;
`design/construction/surveys` owns incremental execution, checkpoints and replay.
Execution limits bound work without narrowing molecular permissions. A supported
target requires a complete witness; exclusion requires exhaustion of its declared
domain. Saved-index integrity and molecular replay remain separate checks.

The detailed decision and its extensions are grouped by responsibility:

- [Execution and molecular admission](0052-resumable-interface-surveys/execution-and-admission.md)
  records branch/member addressing, local populations, primer admission,
  continuation batches, empty-plane proofs and resumable navigation.
- [Conditional auxiliary completion](0052-resumable-interface-surveys/conditional-completion.md)
  records completion objectives, protected sequence groups, preferences, necessary
  endpoint constraints and shared end-pair screening.
- [Checkpoints, coverage and inspection](0052-resumable-interface-surveys/checkpoints-and-inspection.md)
  records storage bindings, queue fairness, replay, conditional snapshots, exact
  selection, atlas retrieval and browser transport.
