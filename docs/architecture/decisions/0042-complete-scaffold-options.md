# Complete scaffold options

A product request may permit many local witnesses without providing a complete
reference. `explore_construction_options` evaluates a bounded prefix of their
recorded Cartesian product. It does not require the caller to choose local
identifiers or declare a preferred completed preparation first. The existing
sole-witness execute contract and reference-bound family matcher remain distinct.

Each needed neighborhood is discovered once under the original request bounds.
Every examined pair, including a rejected continuation, consumes the explicit
1–64 pair budget. Local completion, recorded/evaluated/unexamined pairs and each
continuation's accounting remain separate. The direct-hairpin path has no basal
search. No later ranking, quota increase, relaxation or preference is inferred.

A design identifier binds the authored request and selected local witnesses.
Changing only the continuation budget cannot rename an already evaluated design.
Visible labels are positions within one packet; molecular and preparation
identities remain the existing verified construction identities. Equal geometry
never merges distinct sequences, chemistry or preparations.

`options/` owns orchestration and admission. The ordinary continuation evaluator
in `execution/candidate.py` is shared with family matching; molecular search and
composition remain in their existing owners. Atomic packet publication and
bounded capture share the `packets/` owner; family and options schemas remain
with their respective consumers. The options report
reuses verified portraits, closing crops, stage projections and vector PDF
encoding rather than introducing another molecular renderer.

Saved packets require canonical manifests, bounded inventories, normalized
relative paths and digest agreement. Before grouping or retrieval, admission
replays local and complete authorities, rebinds each outcome to its product
request, verifies deterministic pair membership and rebuilds displayed facts.
Resealing an edited summary or drawing does not confer authority. Retrieval
regenerates drawings from verified records; user-supplied SVG does not reach the
PDF encoder. Publication is create-only and cannot overlap captured sources.

A `hop.scaffold-comparison/v1` request declares distinct desired dimension
combinations and shared properties. `find_construction_option_sets` groups
verified achieved products by the full declared property values and product
stage. It returns all observed backgrounds, per-member alternative labels and
missing members. This is a grouping of an evaluated pool, not a joint search or
proof of global absence. No Cartesian list of all possible selections is built.

`select_construction_options` resolves explicit labels into the existing strict
set contract. The same comparison request can be reused to require exactly one
chosen molecule per intended member and to carry forward its controls. A caller
may instead declare shared and compared properties directly. These two authoring
forms cannot be mixed. Baseline and membership stay explicit. Selected packets
retain ordinary `set.json`, exact preparations and oligo inventory for relocation.

The public workflow is [explore scaffolds](../../guides/explore-scaffolds.md).
Caller studies own fragment-recovery observations, destination checks, and
unfamiliar-user evaluation.
