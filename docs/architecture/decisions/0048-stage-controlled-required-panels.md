# Stage-qualified controls, required search and prior-material bindings

An experimental comparison can preserve a released product despite changes in its
hairpin intermediate. Required loop words can also be missed by a search that
only filters a bounded pool afterwards. Finally, agreement among new materials
does not mean reuse of a previously selected oligo.

## Decision

Comparison-brief/v3 makes these intentions separate and explicit:

- Regional controls name hairpin or endpoint stage and whole-set or named-group
  scope. Recorded ancestry identifies retained regions. Sequence, pairing and
  chemistry remain separate; unknown correspondence and singleton equality are
  unassessed. Legacy `shared_properties` still checks the hairpin.
- Member selectors compile into discovery/v3 required targets. Canonical selector
  identity excludes display names; identical requests share tasks and overlapping
  targets reuse completed options. Required tasks precede optional original
  branches under one `max_branches` cap. Per-task local and continuation budgets
  remain explicit. The original permitted domain stays in the request.
- Prior-material reuse verifies and captures one selected preparation, then
  compiles specified roles into existing fixed policies. Existing specifications,
  chemistry and relevant material constraints are checked independently. Product
  reference, agreement among new members and reuse of prior materials retain
  distinct meanings.
- Construction-set/v2 preserves resolved stage-control membership and embedded
  prior-material references. Retrieval replays the complete authorities and checks
  both controls and reused material identities after relocation.

Execution and admission share one scheduling traversal. Saved previews, tasks,
budget use, options and comparison observations must reproduce from their authored
requests and verified inner authorities. A resealed summary is not authority.
An assessed original-domain pool is labeled by the search it actually contains;
it is never credited with targeted work that was not performed.

## Consequences

The opening comparison reports intended molecular controls, prior-material
changes and missing members. Technical coverage remains accessible beneath that
view. A stopped local quota remains incomplete even when all required members
have witnesses. Task scheduling establishes neither global completeness nor a
best preparation. Fixed-source reuse may remove a method while preserving the
possibility of making the same product from a newly designed source.

HOP owns these reusable contracts and renderers. Caller studies own why a panel
matters and the evidence produced from using it. No laboratory equivalence,
physical stock, assembly compatibility or human-usability result follows from
the modeled comparison.
