# Ecosystem ownership boundaries

HOP is the narrow waist between many sources of design intent and many forms of
downstream use.

```text
human design | scripts | enumeration | campaign optimization
                         |
                         v
             HOP semantic contracts
                         |
                         v
       verified encodings and method products
                         |
                         v
 placement | assessment | studies | experimental execution
```

| Owner | Permanent responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| HOP | Hairpin anatomy, bounded discovery, design derivation, named molecular transformations, destination-neutral products, digests, and verification. |
| Campaign or application caller | Candidate objectives, selection, procurement policy, and application interpretation. |
| Composition service | Generic placement of one immutable annotated object into a larger construct and destination-specific assembly checks. |
| Structure-assessment service | Advisory assessment of an exact digest-addressed molecular state. |
| Study and execution system | Biological identity, protocol conditions, controls, runs, observations, and interpretation. |

HOP and a composition service do not compete. HOP describes the object it
makes; the composition service places that object somewhere else. Neither may
rederive the other's internal features.

A destination-neutral `RestrictionDigestProduct` may carry exact cohesive ends.
It becomes an `AssemblyFragment` only after a caller supplies a destination,
orientation, and compatible assembly relation.

See [HOP in a campaign stack](campaign-orchestration.md) and
[destination neutrality](../methods/destination-neutrality.md).
